About ENTERACTION

This is an English-speaking community built around one simple idea: dare to speak, and keep speaking. We're creating a friendly, low-pressure all-English environment to help members build a speaking habit and gradually gain confidence in expressing themselves.

Conversations are held mostly in English — if you're not sure how to say something, feel free to use AI or translation tools for help. What matters isn't perfect English, it's being willing to try.

What We Do

Casual Chats & Life Sharing

Talk about everyday life in English, in a relaxed and easygoing way.

Weekly Themed Discussions

Share ideas and perspectives around a different topic each week.

Short Talks & Personal Stories

Practice expressing yourself in English by sharing your own stories.

Cultural Exchange

Get to know different cultures and broaden your global perspective.

Books & Games Sessions

Practice speaking through English books, games, and other themed activities.

Before each session, we'll share the topic and some simple prep materials in advance so everyone has time to get ready.

How ENTERACTION Helps You Grow

Every ENTERACTION activity is built on these growth principles:

Learn at Your Own Pace

There's no pressure to speak perfectly or immediately — grow your English at whatever pace feels comfortable for you.

Practice by Doing

Build real speaking confidence through free chats, weekly discussions, short talks, and themed activities — not textbook drills.

Grow Together, No Judgment

Everyone's English level is welcome here. We support each other with patience instead of correction or criticism.

Learn From Fellow Members

Pick up new expressions, cultural insights, and confidence by learning alongside members from different backgrounds.

Community Guidelines

Respect Each Other

No criticizing or mocking anyone's English level.

Respect Privacy

Be mindful of privacy when sharing personal experiences.

Keep It Safe & Fun

Let's keep the space safe, comfortable, and fun together.
